# Adventure Works Sales Analysis

## Business Background
Adventure Works Cycles is a multinational manufacturer of bicycles
sold across North America, Europe and Asia. The company wants to grow
market share by targeting its best customers, expanding online
availability and reducing production costs.

## Objective
Analyse internet sales to understand revenue, cost and profit trends,
and find the top products, customers and regions.

## Dataset
Adventure Works Sales Excel workbook: Fact Internet Sales,
Fact Internet Sales New, Product, Customer and [Sales Territory] sheets.

## Tools Used
Excel: lookup formulas, date functions, pivot tables, charts, dashboard

## What I Did
1. Combined Fact Internet Sales and Fact Internet Sales New into one table
2. Looked up Product Name and Unit Price from the Product sheet and
   Customer Full Name from the Customer sheet
3. Created a date from OrderDateKey, then derived Year, Month No,
   Month Name, Quarter, Year-Month, Weekday No, Weekday Name,
   Financial Month and Financial Quarter
4. Calculated Sales Amount, Production Cost and Profit
5. Built a pivot table of Month vs Sales with a Year filter
6. Created charts: yearwise sales (bar), monthwise sales (line),
   quarterwise sales (pie), and Sales vs Production Cost (combo)
7. Added KPIs and charts for products, customers and regions
8. Built an interactive dashboard

## Key Insights
- [Top-selling product or category and its share of sales]
- [Best and worst region]
- [Sales or profit trend by year, quarter or month]
